Biology reference: [Smithsonian National Museum of Natural History, Butterflies](https://www.si.edu/spotlight/buginfo/butterfly) describes wing scales and the coiled proboscis. [Butterflies and Moths of North America species account](https://www.butterfliesandmoths.org/species/Papilio-machaon) supplies identification and a 6.5–7.5 cm wingspan reference. Individual and subspecies variation is expected.

This is a photograph-guided artistic anatomical reconstruction. Scale spacing, fine ridges, ommatidial arrangement, antenna segment count, internal wing-root articulation and body segmentation are modeled approximations, not measurements of the museum specimen or verified microscopic anatomy. Photographic pigment retains some original lighting. No natural-flight simulation is provided.
